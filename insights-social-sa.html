How South Africans Use Social Media — And What It Means for Brands

South Africa has one of the most active online audiences in Africa. Here’s what the platform mix tells us — and how brands can use it to grow.

Social media in South Africa isn’t just entertainment — it’s a primary channel for communication, commerce, and culture. As of 2025, South Africa has approximately 45 million internet users, with over 26 million active social media users — representing more than 40% of the total population.

Platform Usage in South Africa (2025)

The platform landscape shows clear differences in behaviour and audience intent:

  • WhatsApp: ±24 million users — South Africa’s most-used platform for daily communication and business enquiries.
  • Facebook: ±23 million users — Strong for community reach, local businesses, and advertising.
  • Instagram: ±11 million users — Highly popular among urban youth (18–34).
  • TikTok: ±10 million users — Fastest-growing platform driven by Gen Z and mobile-first users.
  • LinkedIn: ±10 million users — Growing B2B platform with strong engagement among professionals.
  • YouTube: ±25 million monthly users — Major platform for long-form content and brand discovery.

Behavioural Insights

South Africans spend an average of 3–4 hours per day online, with approximately 2.5 hours spent on social media platforms. Mobile devices account for over 95% of social media access, making mobile-first content strategy essential.

Research shows that:

  • Over 70% of users use social media to research brands before purchasing.
  • More than 60% engage with brands directly through comments or direct messages.
  • Video content generates significantly higher engagement than static posts.

What This Means for Brands

The South African digital environment is mobile-first, video-driven, and community-focused. Brands that rely on only one platform risk missing significant audience segments.

  • Instagram & TikTok: Culture, visibility, storytelling.
  • LinkedIn: Credibility, partnerships, B2B growth.
  • Facebook: Community presence and paid advertising reach.
  • WhatsApp: Conversion, customer service, and retention.
